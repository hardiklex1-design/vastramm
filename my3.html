01 / WHY MY3

A SMALL MARK WITH A BIGGER IDEA.

MY3 began with something deeply personal. It was inspired by someone from my past who was very valuable to me, and that inspiration became part of the identity I was building.

What began as an inspiration became much more than a name. MY3 became my first step into creating something of my own — a part of my journey, my identity, and the foundation that eventually led to VASTRAMM.

02 / THE SYMBOL

THREE CHARACTERS.
ONE IDENTITY.

The “MY” carries the personal feeling behind the name, while the “3” completes the mark. Its meaning is personal to me, but its journey became bigger than the inspiration that started it.

MY3 taught me that an idea can begin with one person, but grow into something that becomes part of your own story. It was where I learned to create, keep going, and believe in something before anyone else could see it.
