MY3 is the signature mark behind VASTRAMM — a compact identity designed to feel personal, memorable and unmistakably ours.

01 / WHY MY3

A SMALL MARK WITH A BIGGER IDEA.

MY3 represents the identity behind VASTRAMM. It is deliberately short, direct and easy to recognize — a mark that can live on a garment, a label, a website, packaging or a future collection.

Rather than competing with the VASTRAMM name, MY3 acts as its signature: the personal stamp behind the atelier.

02 / THE SYMBOL

THREE CHARACTERS.
ONE IDENTITY.

The “MY” gives the mark a personal feeling, while the “3” gives it a sharp visual ending. Together, MY3 is intentionally simple — a symbol that can be remembered at a glance.

Its minimal form reflects the same design language used throughout VASTRAMM: clean, monochrome, confident and without unnecessary noise.

03 / WHAT MY3 STANDS FOR

PERSONAL.
MINIMAL.
ICONIC.

01 / PERSONAL

MY3 is the signature attached to the creative identity behind the label.

02 / MINIMAL

A compact mark designed to work from a tiny garment label to a full digital experience.

03 / ICONIC

The ambition is simple: make the mark recognizable without needing an explanation.
