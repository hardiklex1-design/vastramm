VASTRAMM BY MY3 is a contemporary Indian label built around the belief that clothing can feel both deeply rooted and completely new.

01 / THE BEGINNING

FROM AN IDEA TO AN ATELIER.

VASTRAMM began with a simple question: what happens when Indian fashion language meets the precision, restraint and attitude of modern streetwear?

The answer is a label built around silhouettes rather than noise — considered pieces, strong proportions and a visual identity that can live in both the physical and digital world.

02 / THE METHOD

FEWER PIECES.
STRONGER IMPACT.

Each release is treated like an atelier project. We focus on fabric, fit, proportion, finishing and the feeling a piece creates when it becomes part of someone's everyday wardrobe.

The collection evolves, but the principle stays constant: make it intentional.
